Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Benchfit is designed to collect as little as possible.

Your data

The data you paste or open is processed only inside your browser. It is never sent to our servers. A draft of each tool's inputs is kept in your browser's local storage so your work survives a page reload; clear it at any time with your browser's site-data settings.

Licence checks

When you activate a Pro licence, your licence key and a browser label are sent to Lemon Squeezy's licence API to confirm the key; it is re-checked every few days. No analysis data is ever included.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. They receive your name, email, billing address and payment details under their own privacy policy; we receive your order details to provide support.

Third parties

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts, which see standard request data such as your IP address. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions or requests about personal data: the support address shown on the licence page.
