What’s new

Every release of Lattent, newest first.

  1. 16 October 2026

    Lab files, explained candidates and a NIST benchmark

    • Drop instrument files anywhere in Studio: CSV, TXT, XY, JCAMP-DX (including compressed), PANalytical XRDML, Rigaku RAS, Bruker UXD, Thermo SPC and CIF. A CIF becomes a simulated diffraction pattern. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
    • Every generated material now has a Why? panel: how often the generator proposed it, its oxidation states, the predicted formation energy and band gap with intervals, and an estimate of its distance from the convex hull built from Alexandria's stable phases of the same system.
    • A technical note on the Validation page: on the 52 NIST StRD nonlinear-regression fits Lattent reaches four or more correct digits in 51, against 45 for SciPy's curve_fit with default settings. The fitter uses improved damping and scouting restarts and is faster than before.
    • Data privacy is now spelled out on the home page, and a Cite Lattent button gives BibTeX and APA references.
  2. 15 October 2026

    Report agent and help guide

    • Autonomous scientific reports: Facet plans the analyses, runs them, checks them and writes the report (methods, results, figures, quality checks, limitations, next steps), offline. With your AI key it drafts the summary and discussion and reviews supplied figures.
    • A Lattent guide on every page answers questions from the help articles and links to the right tool.
    • Facet AI is a visible menu again.
  3. 15 October 2026

    Support centre and a clearer home page

    • A Support centre with search, guides by topic, troubleshooting and billing help.
    • The home page now opens with the problems Lattent solves and how it works in three steps.
    • AI and machine learning moved into the Platform menu; Support joins the navigation.
  4. 15 October 2026

    Facet, pricing and contact

    • The AI lab partner is now Facet. New: Check reviews the whole project for errors, Read a graph turns a picture of a plot into a table, and Ask accepts figures to interpret and critique.
    • A Pricing page with plan comparison, yearly or monthly billing, and prices in US dollars or rupees. Pay by card, PayPal or Apple Pay worldwide, by UPI, cards or net banking in India through Razorpay, or by invoice.
    • A Contact page; messages reach the team by email.
    • Simpler navigation: Platform and Solutions menus, direct links to AI and Pricing, and a compact Resources menu.
  5. 15 October 2026

    AI and machine learning

    • Equation discovery: symbolic regression finds a compact formula for XY data and recognises physical laws such as Arrhenius, Langmuir and Flory–Fox.
    • AutoML: six kinds of model compared by cross-validation, with input importance, partial dependence, composition descriptors for formula columns and calibrated prediction intervals.
    • Calibrated 90% intervals on every formation-energy and band-gap prediction.
    • A new page, the periodic table of Lattent’s AI, with a live lab.
    • Night mode and an account menu in every header.
  6. 15 October 2026

    Navigation, Solutions and What’s new

    • A new site navigation with Platform, Solutions and Resources menus.
    • A page for each field, from nanomaterials to analytical chemistry, linking straight to the right tool, example or Discover view.
    • This release-notes page.
  7. 15 October 2026

    New identity

    • A new mark: an L of lattice points that doubles as the axes of a plot, with a copper point for the discovery.
    • An indigo-and-copper palette, checked for colour-blind safety and contrast, with Space Grotesk headings.
    • A custom 404 page, social previews and home-screen icons.
  8. 15 October 2026

    Discover

    • Search 5.8 million computed crystal structures in the Alexandria database, with a 3D viewer, simulated XRD with Miller indices and CIF export.
    • Identify XRD phases: peaks are matched against every structure of your elements, with lattice refinement and second-phase detection.
    • Generate materials: Lattent’s own masked-diffusion model proposes compositions; property networks predict formation energy (MAE 0.09 eV/atom) and band gap; each candidate is checked for novelty.
  9. 15 October 2026

    Lattent

    • Benchfit is now Lattent. Old links and project files keep working.
  10. 14 October 2026

    Specialist models and a materials focus

    • Lattent’s own shape model recognises 13 families of behaviour (98.4% on held-out data) and suggests the analysis.
    • Quick tools for XRD, band gaps, activation energy, isotherms, tensile tests, peaks and curve fitting.
  11. 14 October 2026

    Accounts, cloud projects and Lab teams

    • Optional sign-in with one-time email links; cloud projects protected by row-level security; shared projects for Lab plans; secure checkout by Lemon Squeezy.
  12. 14 October 2026

    Lattent Studio

    • A workspace with XY, Column and Multiple-variables tables, 35 equations with AICc auto-fit, statistics with post tests, spectra and materials analyses, PCA, clustering, Bayesian optimisation and an AI co-scientist.
  13. 13 October 2026

    First release

    • Browser calculators for dose–response, standard curves, enzyme kinetics and qPCR, checked against SciPy.
