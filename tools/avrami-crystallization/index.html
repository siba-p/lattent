Avrami (JMAK) crystallisation kinetics

Paste relative crystallinity against time from an isothermal DSC run. Lattent fits the Avrami equation directly and reports the exponent n, the half-time of crystallisation and the rate constant, each with a confidence interval.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Avrami exponent n with a 95% confidence interval
  • Half-time of crystallisation t½
  • Rate constant k = ln2 / t½ⁿ
  • A fit you can compare across temperatures

X(t) = 1 − exp(−k tn),   t½ = (ln2 / k)1/n

Questions

What data do I need?

Relative crystallinity X(t) from the integrated exotherm of an isothermal run, in % or as a fraction. Measure time from the start of crystallisation, after any induction time.

What does the exponent n mean?

n combines how nuclei form (all at once or continuously) and how crystals grow (in one, two or three dimensions). Values from about 2 to 4 are typical for polymers, and non-integer values are common.

Why not the ln(−ln(1−X)) plot?

The double-log plot magnifies the noise at very low and very high crystallinity, so it is usually cut to the middle range. A direct fit uses every point.
