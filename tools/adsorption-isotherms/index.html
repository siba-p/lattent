Adsorption isotherms

Paste equilibrium concentration Cₑ and uptake qₑ. Lattent fits Langmuir, Freundlich, Sips and related models by nonlinear regression, ranks them by AICc and reports q_max and affinity constants with confidence intervals.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Langmuir, Freundlich, Sips and kinetic models compared by AICc
  • q_max, K_L, K_F and n with confidence intervals
  • A plain statement of which model the data support
  • Replicates averaged and shown with error bars

Questions

Should I use the linearised Langmuir plot?

No. Linearised plots distort errors and can favour the wrong model. Nonlinear fitting with model comparison is the current recommendation in the adsorption literature.

Can I fit kinetics too?

Yes: paste qₜ against time, and the pseudo-first-order and pseudo-second-order models are included.
