Curve fitting

Paste any XY data. Lattent's shape model, trained on tens of thousands of simulated lab datasets, recognises the kind of behaviour. Then every matching equation is fitted and ranked by AICc.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • The data shape recognised in your browser by Lattent's own model
  • All matching equations fitted and ranked by AICc and Akaike weight
  • Confidence intervals, residuals and runs test for the chosen model
  • Your own equation if none of the 35 fit

AICc = n ln(SS/n) + 2k + 2k(k+1)/(n − k − 1)

Questions

What does the shape model do?

It reads the pattern of your data (saturating, sigmoidal, thermally activated, a spectrum and so on) and narrows 35 equations to the plausible ones, so spurious models do not win by chance.

Can I type my own equation?

Yes, in Studio: choose User-defined equation and type, for example, A*exp(-k*x) + c.
