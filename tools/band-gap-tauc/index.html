Optical band gap (Tauc plot)

Paste a UV–Vis spectrum as wavelength (nm) or photon energy (eV) against absorbance. Lattent builds the Tauc plot, finds the straight part of the absorption edge and extrapolates the band gap with its confidence interval.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Band gap with a 95% confidence interval
  • Direct and indirect, allowed and forbidden transitions
  • The fitted linear region, adjustable by hand
  • The absorption-edge wavelength

(αhν)^{1/r} = A (hν − Eg)

Questions

Direct or indirect?

Use direct allowed (r = 1/2) for ZnO, GaN, perovskites and most quantum dots; indirect (r = 2) for Si, TiO₂ (anatase) and many oxides. If unsure, try both and compare the straightness of the fit.

Can I use absorbance instead of α?

Yes. α is proportional to absorbance for a film of fixed thickness, which scales the plot but does not move the intercept.
