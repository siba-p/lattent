Tensile test analysis

Paste strain (in % or as a fraction) and stress. Lattent finds the elastic region, applies the 0.2% offset method and reports the mechanical properties with a publication-ready stress–strain figure.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Young's modulus from the steepest linear loading region
  • 0.2% offset yield strength (offset adjustable)
  • Ultimate tensile strength and elongation at break
  • Toughness from the area under the curve

σ = E (ε − ε0 − 0.002)

Questions

My modulus is lower than the handbook value

Without an extensometer, crosshead strain includes machine compliance, which lowers the apparent modulus. Compare samples measured the same way.

Can it handle brittle samples?

Yes. For samples with no yield, the yield strength is left blank and the strength and modulus are reported.
