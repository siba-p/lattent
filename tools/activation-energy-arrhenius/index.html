Activation energy (Arrhenius)

Paste temperatures (K) and a thermally activated quantity such as ionic conductivity, diffusion coefficient or a rate constant. Lattent fits the Arrhenius equation directly, without distorting the errors, and reports Eₐ in kJ/mol and eV.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Eₐ in kJ/mol and eV with a 95% confidence interval
  • Pre-exponential factor
  • Nonlinear fit with relative weighting, plus an Arrhenius-style plot
  • A check against Vogel–Fulcher–Tammann for polymer and glassy electrolytes

Questions

Why not fit ln σ against 1/T?

Linearising distorts the error structure. Lattent fits the exponential directly with 1/Y² weighting, which handles data spanning orders of magnitude correctly.

My data curve on an Arrhenius plot

Curvature often means Vogel–Fulcher–Tammann behaviour (polymer electrolytes, glasses) or a change of mechanism. Use Find the best model in Studio to compare.
