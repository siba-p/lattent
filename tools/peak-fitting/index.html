Peak fitting

Paste any spectrum. Lattent subtracts a polynomial baseline, finds the peaks and fits them all at once, reporting position, height, FWHM, area and shape for each.

Free, no sign-up. The analysis runs in your browser; your data is not uploaded.

What you get

  • Automatic peak detection with adjustable prominence
  • Gaussian, Lorentzian or pseudo-Voigt profiles fitted together
  • Positions with uncertainties, FWHM, areas and relative intensities
  • The fitted spectrum, baseline and peak positions in one figure

I(x) = Σ hk [η L(x) + (1 − η) G(x)] + baseline

Questions

Which profile should I use?

Pseudo-Voigt is the safe default for most spectroscopies: it lets each peak be Gaussian, Lorentzian or between.

Peaks are missing

Lower the minimum prominence, or reduce smoothing for very sharp peaks.
