Questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The analysis code runs in your browser and your data stays in that tab, plus an optional draft saved in your browser's local storage. The only network request Benchfit makes is to check a Pro licence key.

How do I know the numbers are right?

Every model is checked against SciPy, the reference scientific Python library, on test datasets. Parameters agree to six significant figures and standard errors to 0.1%. The validation page lists every check.

Can I use the free version for a paper?

Yes. The analysis is identical in the free and Pro versions. Free PNG figures carry a small “Made with Benchfit” mark; Pro removes it, adds high-resolution and vector exports and writes the methods text.

How should I cite Benchfit?

The auto-written methods include a citation line. Otherwise: “Analyses were performed with Benchfit (siba-p.github.io/benchfit).”

Can my lab pay with a purchase order or grant?

Yes. Lemon Squeezy issues a proper invoice with your institution's details and tax ID. For purchase orders, write to us from the licence page.

Is it suitable for GLP or clinical diagnostics?

No. Benchfit is a research tool. It is not validated under 21 CFR Part 11 or GLP and must not be used for diagnostic decisions.

Does it work offline?

Once a tool page has loaded, the analysis needs no connection, so you can keep working on the bus or in a cold room.
