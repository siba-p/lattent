Organise

Data tables shaped for the analysis you need

Choose an XY table for curves and spectra, a Column table for comparing groups, or a Multiple-variables table for compositions, process settings and properties. Paste straight from Excel or an instrument export; replicate columns are recognised automatically.

  • Spreadsheet editing with paste, transpose and CSV import
  • Each table remembers its analyses and graphs

Fit

Fit any model, then let the data choose

Thirty-five equations across materials science, polymers, nanomaterials, chemistry and biology, from Arrhenius and Langmuir to Avrami, Carreau, Hall–Petch and the four-parameter logistic, or type your own. Auto-fit tries every applicable model and ranks them by AICc, so you see what the data actually support.

  • Confidence intervals and confidence bands, derived quantities such as Eₐ in kJ/mol and eV
  • Constraints, weighting, residual plots and runs test
  • Plain-language warnings when a parameter is poorly determined

Characterise

Spectra, diffraction and mechanical tests

Benchfit understands the measurements materials labs run every day. Fit overlapping XRD, Raman or XPS peaks on a fitted baseline; get d-spacings, Scherrer crystallite size and Williamson–Hall strain; extract optical band gaps from UV–Vis; and read modulus, yield, strength and toughness from stress–strain curves.

  • Gaussian, Lorentzian and pseudo-Voigt multi-peak fitting
  • Tauc analysis for direct and indirect transitions
  • Area under the curve, Savitzky–Golay smoothing and derivatives

Compare

Statistics with the checks built in

t tests, Mann–Whitney, one-way ANOVA, Welch's ANOVA and Kruskal–Wallis, with Tukey and Holm–Šidák post tests. Benchfit checks equal variance and normality for you, suggests the right test when an assumption fails, and draws significance stars on the graph.

  • Descriptive statistics, normality tests, Grubbs outliers
  • Effect sizes and confidence intervals, not only p-values
  • Correlation matrices and multiple linear regression

Discover

From your data to the next experiment

Benchfit learns a Gaussian-process model of a property from the experiments you have run, such as the toughness of a polymer blend, the yield of a synthesis or the conductivity of an electrolyte. It shows which inputs matter and proposes the next experiments most likely to improve the result, respecting mixture constraints. Add them to your table, run them, and repeat.

  • Bayesian optimisation with expected improvement, in batches
  • Cross-validated model accuracy, so you know when to trust it
  • PCA, k-means clustering and correlation for exploring datasets

Questions

How is this different from GraphPad Prism or Origin?

Benchfit covers the core of what scientists use those programs for (curve fitting, statistics and publication graphs), adds materials-specific analyses such as XRD, Tauc and tensile tests, plus Bayesian optimisation and an AI co-scientist. It runs in any browser with nothing to install, and costs a fraction of a desktop licence. It does not yet cover everything they do, such as survival analysis or two-way ANOVA; those are on the roadmap.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Calculations run in your browser and projects are stored in your browser. Data is sent only if you use the optional AI chat, and then it goes directly from your browser to the provider you chose, using your own key.

How do I know the numbers are right?

Every analysis is checked against SciPy and NumPy, the reference scientific Python libraries, on test datasets before each release: parameters, standard errors, p-values, Tukey intervals and Gaussian-process predictions. See the validation page.

I have never used curve-fitting software. Where do I start?

Open a worked example close to your field, look at what Benchfit reports, then replace the numbers with yours. Or create a table, paste your data and press Suggest an analysis.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. The built-in suggestions need no key. The chat is optional and uses your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, billed by them at their API rates, usually cents per conversation.

Can my lab pay with a purchase order or grant?

Yes. Lemon Squeezy issues a proper invoice with your institution's details; for purchase orders, write to us from the licence page.

Is it suitable for regulated or clinical work?

No. Benchfit is research software and is not validated for GLP, 21 CFR Part 11 or diagnostic use.
